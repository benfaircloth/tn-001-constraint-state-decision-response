Incident Response Policy

Security incidents must be reported to the security team within one hour of detection.
The security team will assess severity and assign an incident commander within 30 minutes.
Critical incidents require immediate notification to the VP of Engineering and the legal team.
All incident response actions must be documented in the incident log in real time.
A post-incident review must be completed within five business days of resolution.
Root cause analysis must identify contributing factors without assigning individual blame.
