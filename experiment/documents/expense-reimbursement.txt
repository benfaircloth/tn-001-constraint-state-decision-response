Expense Reimbursement Policy

Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the transaction.
Receipts are required for all expenses over $25.
Travel expenses require pre-approval from a director-level manager.
Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $75 per day.
Personal expenses are not eligible for reimbursement under any circumstances.
Reimbursement requests are processed within two pay cycles of approved submission.
