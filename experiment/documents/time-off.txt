Time Off Policy

Employees accrue 15 days of paid time off per year during their first three years.
After three years, accrual increases to 20 days per year.
Time off requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance for absences of three or more days.
Unused PTO may be carried over up to a maximum of five days into the next calendar year.
PTO is not paid out upon voluntary resignation unless required by state law.
Company holidays are separate from PTO and are published at the beginning of each fiscal year.
