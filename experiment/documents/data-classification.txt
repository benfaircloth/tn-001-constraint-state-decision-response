Data Classification Policy

All company data must be classified into one of four tiers: Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted.
Data owners are responsible for assigning and reviewing classification levels annually.
Confidential and Restricted data must be encrypted at rest and in transit.
Access to Restricted data requires explicit approval from the data owner and the security team.
Sharing Confidential or Restricted data with external parties requires a signed NDA and legal review.
Classification labels must be applied to documents, repositories, and storage locations.
